# Interpretation guardrails

The following interpretation constraints were applied throughout the manuscript.

1. Peak-gene association does not equal experimentally validated regulation.
2. UROPA assignment does not identify a causal target gene.
3. Correlation and permutation do not establish causality or directionality.
4. FIMO q <= 0.05 indicates a high-confidence sequence match, not transcription-factor occupancy.
5. Motif-family similarity does not establish TF binding.
6. Motif overlap does not establish TF cooperation.
7. Candidate regulatory modules are computationally prioritized modules, not experimentally validated regulatory networks.
8. GO/KEGG analyses with FDR >= 0.05 were treated as formally negative.
